Privacy Policy / गोपनीयता नीति

Effective date: Set by the operator before launch. प्रभावी तिथि: ऑपरेटर लॉन्च से पहले तय करे। This notice describes what this MedConnect deployment does. The operator must replace the support contact below and obtain local privacy/legal review before inviting the public.

MedConnect is a medicine-tracking and general-information tool, not a medical provider. Do not enter information you do not want the person operating this server to process.

English

Information processed

  • When you create an account: your name, email address, password hash, and the time you accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
  • If you use medicine tracking: medicine names, scheduled times, and dose events. The history view keeps the latest seven days; older events are removed the next time your history is loaded. Account and schedule data otherwise remain until you delete the account.
  • If you request a password reset: your email, a one-time-code hash and expiry, failed-attempt count, and a keyed hash of the request IP address. Codes expire after ten minutes and are removed after they expire when the reset flow or app cleanup next runs.
  • To reduce automated abuse, the app temporarily stores keyed hashes of request IP addresses and request counts for sign-up, login, password-reset, and assistant limits. These are not plain IP addresses and old counters are pruned on later requests.
  • The AI/health assistant receives the question you submit so this server can produce its built-in general-information response. Guest questions are not added to your medicine account or saved by the assistant feature.
  • Speech-to-text is provided by the browser. The browser or its speech-recognition provider may process microphone audio under its own settings and privacy terms. MedConnect does not receive or store the microphone audio; it receives text only when you submit your question.
  • Your browser stores your chosen interface language and local reminder markers. Browser notification and microphone permissions are controlled by you.

Use, access, and disclosure

Information is used to operate account login, medicine schedules, dose history, password resets, abuse prevention, and the assistant response. The operator and hosting, email, DNS, and speech-service providers may process information as needed to provide their services. Do not claim that a provider never keeps technical logs; check your provider settings and contracts.

Retention and deletion

Use Account and Privacy → Permanently delete my account and confirm with your password to remove your account, medicine schedules, dose history, and password-reset records from the live database. The operator must also apply and document a backup-retention period: deletion from a backup is complete only when that backup expires or is securely removed. Temporary abuse-prevention counters expire as their windows are revisited.

Security and contact

The app uses password hashing, signed session cookies, HTTPS in the supplied production setup, and server-side access checks. No internet service is risk-free.
