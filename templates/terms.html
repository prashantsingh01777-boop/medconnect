English

  1. Purpose. MedConnect provides medicine schedule tracking and limited, general health information. The assistant does not diagnose, prescribe, assess an individual patient, or replace a doctor, pharmacist, or emergency service.
  2. Medicines and records. Add only medicines prescribed or otherwise approved for you. Follow the packaging and clinician's instructions. Check recorded schedules for accuracy; the service cannot verify that a dose was taken.
  3. Prescription scanner. Browser OCR may misread a prescription and does not reliably interpret handwriting or dosing instructions; verify every medicine and time with the original prescription before saving.
  4. Reminders. Browser alarms depend on an open page, working device, audio permission, and network/browser behavior. Background suspension, battery-saving features, notification settings, a device being off, or outages can delay or prevent a reminder. Never rely on this app as your only medication or safety reminder.
  5. Caregiver alerts, custom messages, and adherence reports. Alerts and user-composed SMS/email messages depend on configured providers, recipient consent, network availability, and provider rules; delivery may fail or be delayed and charges may apply. You are responsible for the message and must send it only to people who agreed to receive it. Do not use messages for emergencies. The adherence report is calculated from schedules and user-entered dose records, uses a 30-minute on-time window, and may not reflect whether a dose was actually taken. It is not medical advice.
  6. Assistant and speech input. Assistant responses are limited reference information, may be incomplete, and are not tailored medical advice. Speech-to-text may use an external browser provider. Review recognized text before sending it.
  7. Emergency dial link. The SOS link opens your device's phone dialer; you must place the call. Availability, charges, and whether 108 is the right local service depend on your carrier and location.
  8. Accounts and deletion. Keep your login credentials private. You may delete the account and app records in Account and Privacy after re-entering your password. Encrypted/offline backups may remain until their documented retention period ends.
  9. Availability and changes. The operator may update, suspend, or discontinue the service. No uptime, reminder delivery, or uninterrupted access is promised by this self-hosted starter setup.
