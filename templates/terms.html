English

  1. Purpose. MedConnect provides medicine schedule tracking and limited, general health information. The assistant does not diagnose, prescribe, assess an individual patient, or replace a doctor, pharmacist, or emergency service.
  2. Medicines and records. Add only medicines prescribed or otherwise approved for you. Follow the packaging and clinician's instructions. Check recorded schedules for accuracy; the service cannot verify that a dose was taken.
  3. Prescription and pill-photo tools. Browser OCR may misread a prescription and does not reliably interpret handwriting or dosing instructions; verify every medicine and time with the original prescription before saving. Pill-color analysis is approximate and cannot identify a medicine. Ask a pharmacist or clinician when uncertain.
  4. Reminders. Browser alarms depend on an open page, working device, audio permission, and network/browser behavior. Background suspension, battery-saving features, notification settings, a device being off, or outages can delay or prevent a reminder. Never rely on this app as your only medication or safety reminder.
  5. Caregiver alerts and adherence reports. Caregiver notifications depend on operator-configured messaging providers and recipient consent; they may fail, be delayed, or incur charges. The adherence report is calculated from schedules and user-entered dose records, uses a 30-minute on-time window, and may not reflect whether a dose was actually taken. It is not medical advice.
  6. Assistant and speech input. Assistant responses are limited reference information, may be incomplete, and are not tailored medical advice. Speech-to-text may use an external browser provider. Review recognized text before sending it.
  7. Emergency dial link. The SOS link opens your device's phone dialer; you must place the call. Availability, charges, and whether 108 is the right local service depend on your carrier and location.
  8. Emergency location alerts. If configured, you must explicitly authorize location access and confirm that each listed caregiver agreed to receive your location before sharing starts. Your browser may also show its own location-permission prompt; sharing proceeds only if you allow it. Your current location is sent to the listed caregivers by SMS and WhatsApp, with updates every 15 minutes until you stop or the selected duration (15 minutes to 24 hours) ends. GPS access, an open browser page, network coverage, messaging-provider configuration, and provider acceptance are required; messages can be delayed, fail, or incur charges. You can stop sharing in the app, and logging out or removing the last caregiver stops future updates. This feature does not contact emergency services automatically, dispatch help, verify delivery, or guarantee safety. For urgent help, call your local emergency service directly.
  9. Accounts and deletion. Keep your login credentials private. You may delete the account and app records in Account and Privacy after re-entering your password. Encrypted/offline backups may remain until their documented retention period ends.
  10. Availability and changes. The operator may update, suspend, or discontinue the service. No uptime, reminder delivery, or uninterrupted access is promised by this self-hosted starter setup.
