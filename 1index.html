👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Caregiver alerts

If a dose is not marked taken 15 minutes after its scheduled time, the server will try to send SMS and WhatsApp alerts to your saved contacts. This requires the app server to be running and the operator to configure Twilio. Messages may be delayed or fail; this is not an emergency service.

Use international format, for example +919876543210.
